# Known Limitations

Surface detection is experimental. YouTube, Reddit, Instagram, X, and TikTok can change UI labels and view hierarchies without notice.

v0.1 implements experimental matchers for:

- YouTube Shorts
- Reddit Popular / All

Other surfaces are represented in rule packs and UI but require additional matchers before they should be treated as reliable.

Google Play distribution is not a v0.1 target. AccessibilityService policy review should happen before any Play Store release.
